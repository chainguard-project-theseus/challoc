## Whole-allocator theorems

All six are **to port**: each was proved on the prototype, and every one runs through `heap/` and `api/` (Phase B, lane c). The descriptions below are the prototype's, kept as the target; the per-CPU parts no longer apply, since upstream removed the per-CPU mode.

The proofs so far establish per-function contracts. These theorems cover sequences of calls across all threads. They were Phase 0 of the original Lean plan — an abstract heap of live blocks — and were set aside when verification moved into the Verus code.
State them in Verus as a ghost model over the real allocator: the building blocks now exist. A Lean model of the slab protocol, extending `src/model/`’s bounded checker to unbounded threads, remains an option for concurrency.

- **Region separation (to port):** Proved in both cache modes (thread caches and per-CPU caches), pointer authentication on or off. `malloc`, `calloc`, `realloc` (a moved block), `aligned_alloc`/`memalign`, `valloc`, `pvalloc`, `mallocx`, `rallocx` (a moved block), `challoc_malloc_usable`, `challoc_calloc_usable`, `challoc_realloc_usable` (a moved block) and the `operator new` family ensure `heap::fits`: a non-null result is a `Regions::user_block`, a slot start of a class at least the size or a page start in the huge region with the size inside it. `heap::theorem_region_separation` turns that into "in a class region or the huge region, never in a gap, no byte in metadata". The slot fact flows from `SlabTable::slot_ptr` through `ClassState::claim`, `Shared::alloc_direct` and the cache stacks, whose invariant `CachePerms::tracks` (each entry belongs to one header and index; a header's first `len` entries stand for slots of its class, through `pac::auth` with the stack base as the modifier when entries are signed: `heap::plain`) every verified push, pop, refill, flush, run rebuild and fresh stack keeps; the free paths push only what `Regions::slab_slot`/`slot_in_class` proved a slot. The per-CPU caches keep the same facts in ghost state that all threads share: an `AtomicInvariant` (`heap::percpu::PcpuInv`, built by `Percpu::build`) holds the view of every per-CPU header (`rseq::Cells`: capacity, stack, the plain addresses of its first `len` entries, and a bound on `zeroed`), and its predicate (`PcpuPred`, `hdr_ok`) says each entry is a slot of the header's class. Each rseq section is one trusted atomic step on that state (`rseq::pop_at`, `push_at`, `batch_at`, `taken_at`, `installed_at`); `Percpu::pop_in`, `push_in`, `batch_in`, `take_in` and `install_in` open the invariant around it and prove the predicate kept, and `Heap::percpu_alloc` and the per-CPU slow paths ensure `heap::percpu::served`. **Trusted:** the kernel's `mmap` contract behind `Regions::reserve`, which verifies against `from_parts`' preconditions (`linux::try_reserve`: a successful reservation is not at address 0 and ends below `sys::pac::user_va_limit`, the kernel's default map window, which `axiom_user_va_limit` puts at or below `2^48`, and carries a `Reserved` token, and `Reserved::is_disjoint` keeps two live tokens apart) and the page at least 4 KiB (a run-time check in `Regions::reserve_owning`; `api::init` passes the kernel's page); `api::init` handing `reserve`'s result to `Shared`; the new conjuncts of `Shared::wf` (separated regions, the huge base and page, the `PUSH_PAC` bit as `heap::pac_mode`) and `Heap::wf` (`CachePerms::tracks`, `heap::regions_ok`), assumed wherever a trusted accessor hands out a `Shared` or a `Heap` and kept by the unverified cache writers; `fast_flags`' `PUSH_PAC` bit; `Heap::alloc_slow`'s slot result (the composition of its verified callees); `process_regions` (one `Shared` per process) and the `SlabTable` invariant tying each table to it; the rseq sections' atomic-step contracts (`src/rseq.rs`, each with the instructions that justify it), which assume the calling thread's registered rseq area and that only the sections write the per-CPU headers' modelled fields and stacks; `percpu::axiom_tables_apart` (the two tables `Percpu::build` reserves do not overlap); the per-CPU tables reading zero when `Percpu::build` reserves them; `take_all`'s count fitting its buffer (its callers recompute the capacity from the configuration and do not prove `cap <= out.len()`); `claim_with`'s claim that no claim wins a padding bit; `stack_mut`, `axiom_fresh_stack`, `cache_ptr` and `other_caches`' header classes; element preservation of `sort_unstable`, `select_nth_unstable` and their keyed forms (`src/vspec/slice.rs`); `sys::pac::axiom_auth_sign`, the PAuth round trip (`autda` of `pacda` of an address below `user_va_limit` with the same modifier gives it back; the identity on x86_64), with `sign` and `auth` specified as `spec_sign` and `spec_auth`, one function per process, which assumes the program never resets its data key with `prctl(PR_PAC_RESET_KEYS)`. **Missing:** `realloc`/`rallocx` keeping a huge run state only `libc::kept` (a page start of the huge region, aligned), not `size` bytes of the region, because the huge descriptors state no bound of `usable` against the region (an `Inner::wf` conjunct `state == Active ==> start * page + usable <= npages * page` would give it; a slot kept in place `fits`); `posix_memalign`'s out-pointer store is outside the model; `mallocx` without an alignment flag is stated at alignment 1, not the quantum.
- **Fit and alignment (to port):** Proved with region separation: `heap::fits(p, size, align)` puts `size` bytes in the slot's class or the huge region, and `p % align == 0`, with `align` the quantum for `malloc`, `calloc`, `realloc` and `operator new`, the requested power of two for `aligned_alloc`, `alloc_aligned` and aligned `new`, and `1 << lg_align` for `mallocx`/`rallocx`. Slot alignment is `Regions::lemma_slot_aligned` (class bases `MAX_SLAB`-aligned, slabs powers of two no smaller than their slots, `class_for`'s multiple), huge alignment `Huge::alloc_run`'s `spec_run_at` (from `find`/`align_page`) plus the page for small alignments; every class size is a multiple of the quantum (`layout::lemma_class_size_quantum`). `valloc`/`pvalloc` are stated at the page (`api::page_size` ensures `1 << lg_page` of the process's regions). **Missing:** the huge fit is "`size` bytes of the region from a run's first page", not the run descriptor's `usable`.
- **Invalid free (to port):** Proved in every configuration for the block shape: `free`, `free_sized`, `free_aligned_sized`, `realloc`, `reallocarray`, `realloc_usable`, `free_usable`, `dallocx`, `sdallocx`, `rallocx`, `malloc_usable_size`, `sallocx`, `xallocx` and the `operator delete` family ensure `heap::free_target(p)` of every non-null `p` they return for (a slot start of some class, or a page start in the huge region), so any other pointer aborts (`Shared::classify_free`, `Regions::classify`, whose `Huge` result is an exact page start, `old_size_or_keep`/`realloc_old`). The metadata writers on the free paths require the target before they write: `Heap::push_cache`/`push_cache_flags` (`heap::slot_start`), `push_fast`, `push`, `free_slow`, `free_other`, `free_slow_in`, `percpu_free_slow` and `zero_slot` (the slot of their class), `Shared::free_direct` (bitmap release) and `Shared::free_huge` (`page <= huge_pages()`, the index `classify_free` computed). **Assumed, not proved:** liveness. A slot start whose bit is clear aborts on the exact paths only by the run-time bit tests of `check_located`/`check_live`; the sized fast path pushes a slot start of the hinted class untested (the flush catches it); a huge page start that is not a live run aborts in `Huge::free_run`; that a slot the program passes was allocated to it and not freed since is the C caller's contract. The per-CPU pushes (`Percpu::push_in`, `batch_in`) require the slot fact, which the per-CPU invariant keeps. A thread's first `free` may create its heap (`heap_for_thread`) before the abort. **Missing:** a ghost model of live blocks (Uniqueness) to state the double-free half.
- **Metadata integrity (to port):** Proved in both cache modes: `heap::theorem_metadata_integrity` puts a non-null block that `heap::fits` describes apart from the metadata the allocator keeps in the metadata region. The `Shared` static (the lock values of `Meta`, the huge allocator and the class tables, arena 0, the statistics) and thread-local storage are not in that region and not covered. The footprint is `Regions::in_meta`, the metadata region, which `Regions::separated` keeps apart from the class and huge regions (`Regions::lemma_user_block_apart`). Its members: every range the process's `Meta` hands out (`Meta::holds`, ensured by `alloc`, `alloc_zeroed`, `reserve`, `reserve_committed` and `reserve_table`; `Shared::wf` ties `Meta::spec_base`/`spec_len` to the regions, which `Meta::owning` ensures); `Meta`'s own writes, the free-list link words (`meta::Inner::lemma_links_in_region`, an `Inner::wf` conjunct, stated on the lock's value and so not a conjunct of the theorem) and the reused object `alloc_zeroed` clears; each class table's descriptors, owner words and bitmap words, the last when the bit count is a multiple of 64 (`SlabTable::meta_ok`, which `SlabTable::new` proves from `Lazy::reserve`; `desc_addr`, `owner_addr`, `bit_word_addr`); every well-formed heap's cache headers and stack entries (`cell_ok` includes `cell_in_meta`; `Heap::ensure_cache` proves it of each stack it allocates; `Heap::lemma_caches_in_meta`); and every live huge descriptor (`perms_ok`, from `alloc_desc`), descriptor table slot (`Table::storage`) and free-page bitmap word (`Set::new`, `TwoLevelBitmap::place`) of a value the huge lock protects (`huge::Inner::lemma_in_meta`). In the per-CPU mode, also every header of both per-CPU tables, and every entry of an installed per-CPU stack in each value the per-CPU invariant admits (`Percpu::lemma_in_meta`, `lemma_stack_in_meta`; `Percpu::build` and `Heap::percpu_ensure` take them from `Meta`). **Trusted:** `vspec::ptr::axiom_ptr_add_addr` (`p.add(i)` is `i` elements past `p` inside its allocation, for a range that does not wrap); `axiom_meta_lock_frame` fixes the region's bounds along with the page and ghost identities; `Shared::put_head` storing the `Meta` it is given (from which `Shared::init` proves the `Shared::wf_built` conjuncts for the meta bounds and every table's `meta_ok`, through `Meta::new` (`Meta::owning` with an assumed `Unbacked` token for the region, trusted) and `SlabTable::new`); the `place` ensures of the trusted `TwoLevelBitmap::from_raw` and `from_raw_chunked`; the trusted heap creation and `Heap::other_caches`, whose headers join `Heap::wf` and so claim a place in the metadata region; the trusted class accessors (`desc_unchecked`, `bits_unchecked`, `is_set_unchecked`, `owner_unchecked`, `owner_word`), which compute the addresses `desc_addr`, `bit_word_addr` and `owner_addr` name; `axiom_fresh_block`, `axiom_moved_block` and `axiom_slot_writable` for the user-block writes (`calloc`'s fill and `realloc`'s copy write at most the `size` bytes `fits` places, `zero_slot` one slot of its class, but the axioms grant validity for any length). **Missing:** writes through `&` references to atomics are located, not tied to permissions: descriptors, owner words and `AtomicBitmap` words (the Uniqueness work would carry them); the arenas' slab-set tables (`SetTable::new` and `Set::in_table` do not state their placement), the arena and heap structs themselves and the background thread's stack beyond `Meta::holds`; a class bitmap whose bit count is not a multiple of 64 ends its last word in the page padding past the reserved length (`SlabTable::lemma_bit_word_in_meta` requires whole words; `Meta` rounds tables to pages, so the word is mapped); `Shared::zero_huge` clears `usable` bytes that no huge invariant bounds by the run; `Meta::free` and `Meta::commit` callers' claims.
- **Uniqueness (to port):** Each slot belongs to exactly one of the bitmap’s free set, one thread’s cache, or the program; no slot is handed out twice. The design and the proved prefix are in "Uniqueness: slot tokens" below. **Missing:** tokens through the flush, the slow paths and the per-CPU caches. **Size:** Weeks; the flagship theorem.
- **Zeroed memory (to port):** Proved in both cache modes (thread caches and per-CPU caches). `libc::calloc_zeroed` (the body of `calloc`) ensures a non-null result comes with a `vspec::zero::Zeroed` token for its `n * m` bytes; `jemalloc::mallocx_zeroed` the same for `size` bytes under `MALLOCX_ZERO`; `jemalloc::rallocx_zeroed` a token for a new block's bytes past the `k` it copied; `libc::calloc_usable_zeroed` for slab-class requests. A token says its bytes read as zero while its holder owns them; tokens come only from `vspec::zero::write_zero` (the skipped-or-not `memset`), from the kernel axioms `vspec::sys::axiom_fresh_anon_zero` (`mmap`: anonymous memory reads zero until written) and `axiom_discarded_zero` (`MADV_DONTNEED`), and from splitting and joining. The slab path: `ClassState::claim_zero` returns one token per slot of a zeroed claim (`class::claim_zero_ok`, shuffled with `claim_random_zero`); the refill and the run rebuild put them in `CachePerms::zero` under the stack entries they fill (`heap::zero::lemma_install_claim`), `CachePerms::zero_ok` (a conjunct of `Heap::wf` and `Heap::keeps`) keeps a token for each of a header's first `min(len, zeroed)` entries through every verified header writer (push clips, pop and the scheduled pop move tokens, flush drops the prefix), and a pop from inside the prefix hands its token to `CachePerms::handed`, which `Heap::take_handed` gives to `heap::alloc_zeroed` and `mallocx::alloc`. The huge path: `Huge::take` makes the run's token (`zero_run`: pages at or past the mark are fresh; a `Purged` extent while `purged_is_zero` read true is discarded; a run past a `Purged` extent that touches the mark is both) and `alloc_run`, `Shared::alloc_huge`, `Heap::alloc_huge` and `libc::alloc_huge` pass it on, for `Huge::spec_usable(size)` bytes. The per-CPU path: the per-CPU invariant keeps a token for each of a header's first `min(len, zeroed)` entries (`hdr_ok`); `Percpu::batch_in` moves a zero claim's tokens in when a batch lands on an empty stack, `pop_in` takes the popped entry's token out when the section reports it zero, and the per-CPU paths hand it to `CachePerms::handed`. A pop never lowers `zeroed`; `push` and a `push_batch` onto a non-empty stack clip it, and `percpu::lemma_batch` needs that clip (PR #4): with the earlier `push_batch` the proof fails at `Percpu::batch_in`. **Trusted:** the two kernel axioms and their call sites' protocol premises (a `Fresh` slab, pages past the huge mark, and `Meta` objects above the bump pointer or newly committed tables were never written; `Purged` is set only in the lock hold that flushes its `MADV_DONTNEED`, with no allocated slot or run), and `table::axiom_zero_entry` (all-zero bytes are an empty descriptor table entry); `claim_with`'s token ensures, the composition of `take`'s zero decision (`Fresh`, or `Purged` with `purged_is_zero`, bits `0..n` claimed in the same hold by `claim_prefix`) and `finish` clearing the flag for any other pick, which carry no tokens themselves; `alloc_slow`'s handed token and `Heap::wf` for heaps a trusted accessor hands out; that the only writes verified code makes to a token's bytes are the ones the proof accounts for (the `memset`s, which write zeros, and `realloc`'s copy into a fresh block's prefix, which `rallocx_zeroed` splits off). **Bugs found:** under the per-CPU mode `rseq::push_batch` onto a non-empty stack left `zeroed` above `len` (a pop does not lower it), so a dirty batch pushed onto a partly consumed zero batch was popped as zero and `calloc` skipped its `memset`; PR #4 clips it, and `heap::linux_tests::percpu_dirty_batch_onto_a_zero_prefix_is_not_zero` tests it. On x86_64, `rseq::pop_raw` compared the new `len` with `zeroed` after its commit, outside the critical section: a thread preempted there read a `zeroed` that another thread on its id wrote after popping the stack empty and pushing a zero batch, and reported its own dirty slot zero, so `calloc` skipped its `memset`; PR #5 loads `zeroed` before the commit, as aarch64 does. `rseq::tests::pop_zero_report_survives_a_refill_after_the_commit` drives the race with a per-thread timer signal: it failed in under a second before the fix and passes 15 million pops after it. Written against the earlier asm (the zero bit unclaimed on x86_64), the per-CPU `calloc` proof fails at the four pop exits (`percpu_alloc`, `percpu_pop_plain`, `percpu_alloc_slow`, `percpu_run_pop`). **Missing:** tokens through `take`, `plan` and `finish`; `calloc_usable`'s huge `memset` length (`usable_size` is not tied to the run); `rallocx`'s `k` is not tied to the old block's usable size.

All six assume sequential consistency (Tier 4) and that the kernel honors `mmap`, `madvise`, and `mprotect`.

### Uniqueness: slot tokens

**Tokens.** `SlabTable::new` makes one `vstd` `GhostSetAuth<int>` per class table, and names it the class's authority (`table::slot_loc`). Its elements are the class's slot addresses: bit `slab * slab_bits + slot` of the class bitmap maps to its slot's address (`bitmap::Slots::addr`), one to one on slots (`bitmap::lemma_addr_inj`); padding bits have no element. A token is a `GhostSubset<int>` or `GhostSingleton<int>` of that authority. Two tokens of one authority are disjoint, so no address is in two of them.

**Where a slot's token lives.**
- Bit clear (free): in the `AtomicInvariant` of its bitmap word. `bitmap::WordPred` holds the word's permission and the tokens of exactly its clear slots. `SlabTable::word_invs` holds every word's invariant, and each `AtomicBitmap` view carries a reference to them and its slot 0's bit.
- Claimed: with the claimer. `AtomicBitmap::claim_word` and `claim_from` move the tokens of the bits their `fetch_or` won out of the word. `claim_prefix`, `take` and `plan` hand a zero pick's prefix to `finish` by pick index, and `finish` returns one `GhostSingleton` per written pointer, keyed by index (`class::SlotToks`).
- In a thread cache: in `heap::CachePerms::toks`, keyed by stack entry. `CachePerms::tokened` says each of a header's first `len` entries holds its slot's token, of the header's class's authority. `Heap::refill` moves the claim's tokens onto the stack with their slots (reversed), `Heap::pop_top` hands the top entry's token out, and `Heap::push_cache` puts the pushed slot's token on.
- Live: with the caller. A C caller holds no ghost state, so the `free` contract stands in: `class::axiom_free_token` gives the token of the slot the program frees, at the program's free paths in `heap/` ("whoever frees a block owns it").
- Released: `ClassState::release_owned` takes a bag of the batch's tokens (a `GhostSubset` of distinct addresses), and `AtomicBitmap::release` hands each word's back. A batch with one slot twice cannot be given its tokens. Because it must hand them over, its `fetch_and` provably finds every bit it clears set, so `release_word`'s double-free abort is unreachable under the contract.

**Atomics.** `vstd::atomic_ghost` compiles every operation as `SeqCst`. `src/vspec/word.rs` instead wraps the two `core` `AtomicU64` operations that change bitmap words (`fetch_or`, `fetch_and`) as `#[verifier::atomic]`, `#[inline(always)]` functions over a ghost `WordPerm` (vstd's `PAtomicU64` pattern), passing the caller's ordering through; loads stay plain `core` loads, whose vstd result is any value. Per-function disassembly of the release library matches upstream's on x86_64 and aarch64, with immediates and addresses masked (the lane's `disasm-check.sh` and `disasm-check-arm.sh`, outside the repository).

**Trusted.**
- Each wrapped `core` operation is one atomic operation on the word, and the proof model is sequentially consistent: the wrappers' specifications ignore the ordering.
- `word::word_addr` is a word's identity; `SlabTable::bits_unchecked`'s address arithmetic makes its view's words the class bitmap's words `first / 64 ..`, for a slab below the watermark, which never passes `nslabs` (not stated as a precondition: `bits` checks only the watermark).
- `table::axiom_fresh_words`: the table `Meta::reserve_table` has just returned reads zero. It is private to `table.rs`, and `SlabTable::new` calls it once, for its own bitmap; a second permission for one word would let a proof write the word outside its invariant.
- `table::axiom_slot_loc`: `SlabTable::new` runs once per class in a process (`Shared::init`), as `process_regions` assumes, so every arena's refills of a class draw on one authority. Two calls for one class would prove `false`; it is private to `table.rs`, with `SlabTable::new` its one call.
- `AtomicBitmap::init` keeps the word invariants. It is the only bitmap writer outside the wrappers, and it runs once per slab, at the slab's first hand-out, when its bits are still clear. Its padding writes bypass the permissions, which agree with the words on slot bits only; the invariants read nothing else.
- `class::axiom_no_padding_won`: no claim wins a padding bit, so every pointer `finish` writes is a slot start with its token (`SlotToks::owns`). Private to `class/mod.rs`; `ClassState::claim_with`, whose body verifies, is its one call. Its loop is `exec_allows_no_decreases_clause`: each round reserves a slot only while `live`, an atomic releasers lower without the lock, says so.
- `class::axiom_free_token`: the `free` contract, one call per free at the program's free paths. Two calls for one slot while its token exists elsewhere prove `false`, so it is exactly as sound as the contract. `release_owned` does not call it: it takes the batch's tokens from its caller.

**Proved.** `claim_word`, `claim_from` and `release` move exactly the tokens of the bits they change. `claim_prefix`, `take` and `plan` return the zero picks' tokens. `finish` returns the token of every pointer whose bit is a slot, and `claim_with` threads them across its rounds (verified; that every won bit is a slot is `axiom_no_padding_won`). `ClassState::claim_tokens` passes `claim_with`'s tokens through, and `claim_random_zero` carries them through the shuffle (`class::shuffle_zero` swaps each slot's token with it). `release_owned`, `release_slab` and `release_word` hand each word's tokens back with its `fetch_and` (`class::scratch_bits`), from the bag the caller gives. With the signed-entry mode off, `Heap::refill` (without `slot_randomize`), `pop_fast`, `push_fast` and the three `free` fast paths keep `Heap::tokened`, and `Heap::alloc_fast_tokens` returns the popped slot's token.

**Where the chain stops.** `Heap::tokened` holds of a heap whose caches are empty and survives the paths above, but nothing establishes it for a new heap (heaps are built outside the proofs) and no other cache writer keeps it: `alloc_slow` (trusted), `free_slow`, `flush`, `rebuild_run`, the GC and the node caches do not carry tokens. `malloc` calls `alloc_fast`, which drops the popped token (the `free` contract conjures it back). The per-CPU caches are untouched.

**Next.**
- The flush: its `select_nth_unstable` and `sort_unstable` need permutation specifications (`src/vspec/slice.rs` states element preservation), so the tokens of the flushed prefix can follow their pointers into `release_owned`, which takes them (the heap's call sites pass `Tracked::assume_new()` until then).
- `alloc_slow` and `free_slow`: verify them, or state `tokened` in their trusted contracts.
- Discharge `axiom_no_padding_won`: give each word invariant the slabs whose padding it has set, and prove "handed out" from the watermark with a wrapped `committed_slabs` whose invariant holds persistent witnesses (`GhostPersistentSubset`).

## Tier 4 — beyond the current tooling

- Check the `Relaxed`, `Acquire`, and `Release` orderings with a weak-memory tool such as loom or GenMC; the Verus proofs assume sequential consistency.
- Use a liveness-capable model for lock waits, per-CPU retries, and `claim_with`; their progress depends on other threads, and 13 functions use `exec_allows_no_decreases_clause`.
- Model the kernel and CPU boundary before removing trust from roughly 30 asm and syscall wrappers, including `sys/raw.rs`’s `syscall0`–`syscall6`, TLS, rseq, CPUID, PAC, and `cldemote`.
- Revisit `config::TABLE`, mallctl readers, print sinks, and `fmt::Display` implementations when Verus can handle their function-pointer and formatting patterns; that support would open these bodies to verification.
