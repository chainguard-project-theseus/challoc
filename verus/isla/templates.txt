# The asm! blocks the Isla records discharge (verus/ISLA.md), verbatim up
# to whitespace: FILE<TAB>FN<TAB>ASM. run.sh fails when no asm! call in
# fn FN reads ASM, so a changed template fails until its record is
# revisited.
