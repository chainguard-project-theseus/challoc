## Notes for the human

- What did not move: the fork cost itself. The metadata VMA count was the smaller part of `connect20`; the probe's cap curve shows that even one writable mapping for the whole region (15 GiB) buys 10-15% of `fork()` on aarch64 and nothing on x86_64, because the child's cost is page-table sparsity, not VMA count: every touched table head is one page in its own PTE page (2 MiB apart or more), so VmPTE stays at 1.3 MiB against glibc's 120 KiB whatever the cap, and the 105 class-region lines (52 committed slabs, 53 tails) are the other half of the mappings. The +2% same-VM `connect20` is what the 8 MiB cap is worth; the remaining +43% / +59% against glibc is structural to per-class regions plus per-class tables and is what the tuning doc now says a pooler is for. The layout that would remove it is a slab-major interleave of the descriptor, bitmap and owner tables (slab `k` of every class in one row, so a fresh process's touched heads pack into a few pages), which changes the `free` path's address arithmetic (`bits_base + slab * slab_bits / 8`, `desc_base + slab * stride`) and is lane A's call under the full protocol, not a metadata-commit change.
- The child's 58 slab commits: worth a follow-up, but not as a metadata change. They are `commit_slab` calls in the class regions for a heap whose classes start empty in the child (the postmaster's partial slabs are its own). Two candidates, both in the class commit path (lane B's `SlabTable::commit_slab`): commit a fresh arena's first group per class in one `mprotect` (a group is 64 KiB..2 MiB, one call instead of one per slab) or let the child inherit the parent's partial slabs through the arena instead of claiming fresh ones. Either needs a `connect20` A/B and the per-slab `mprotect` count from `strace -f` on the VM host (attaching from inside the container is refused).
- x86_64 `make test` has one failure independent of this lane (`sys::auxv::linux_tests::keys_match_libc_and_the_vector`, `AT_HWCAP` from the raw vector 529267711 against `getauxval` 6): it fails on `main` f419b88 built on the same VM in the same container. Probably `getauxval(AT_HWCAP)` under Wolfi's glibc 2.44 on this kernel returning something other than the vector's word; for the owner of `sys/auxv.rs`.
- glibc's `connect20` list on the aarch64 lane VM is bimodal (1927 1941 2181 2229 2347): the +43.2% median is against 2181; against the low pair it would be +27%. The same-VM challoc pair is the number to trust for this change.
