## Notes for the human

- The lock test hang was the test, not the lock. Reproduced on the host by delaying the waiter's start past 20 ms: the test asserted `state == CONTENDED` at a fixed 20 ms after spawning the waiter; under load the waiter had not been scheduled (or was still in the spin phase), the assertion panicked with the lock still held, and `std::thread::scope` joins its threads on the way out even when the body panicked, so the test thread parked in the join (`Thread::park`, a futex on Linux) while the waiter parked in `futex_wait` on a lock nobody would release: two threads in `futex_wait`, and the panic message held by the harness until the test ends, which is never. The lock's protocol has no gap here: a waiter that parks after the release gets `EAGAIN` from `futex_wait` on the changed word and takes the lock on the re-swap (`lock_slow_with`). The test now uses a detached waiter, polls for the announce with a 10 s bound, reads the word and the waiter's progress, releases, then asserts, and waits for the wake with the same bound; a waiter that never announces fails at 10.00 s naming the hint (verified with a 15 s delayed waiter).
- `release_owned` codegen (aarch64, `scripts/codegen-diff.sh` base f419b88 vs f16ecc9): q16 208 to 220 instructions, q8 198 to 210, both variants otherwise `exact 131, reloc-only 403`; `.text` +0x40 bytes each. On the executed slab-switch path the change is +4 instructions: the descriptor's `mul; ldrb state` became `madd; ldrh w10,[desc+arena]; ldrh w8,[self+arena]; cmp; b.ne cold; ldrb state` (the `add` folded into the `madd`), so one extra load from the descriptor line the `Fresh` check already reads, one from `self` (already in cache), a compare and a not-taken branch, once per slab switch, never per pointer. The other +8 are the cold abort's argument setup (`slab >> lg_group`, the two arenas). The second "changed" function, `__aarch64_ldset8_acq` 12 to 16, is four `nop`s of alignment padding after the grown function, not a code change. Nothing inlines `release_owned` (it is the one changed function), so the flush and per-CPU callers are untouched.
- Quick rows to watch: the check runs once per slab switch in every batch flush, so the rows with many small flushes of few-slot runs are the sensitive ones: valkey1 and the sized-free paths (the sized path defers its checks to this exact block, design 6.3), and xmalloc-test; larson batches are long runs per slab and should not move. If a row moves beyond the band, the four-instruction slab-switch cost is the only mechanism; the commit f16ecc9 stands alone and can be dropped.
- The per-CPU release path needs no separate check: every release in `heap/percpu.rs` (the refill overflow at 521 and 598, the GC at 741 and 758, the drain at 799) goes through `Shared::release_routed`, which calls `release_owned` per owner run, so the compare covers them. One stale comment in `heap/shared.rs` `release_routed` (not this lane's file): its panic comment says "the descriptor says it is" where `arena_of_slot` reads the owner word (`SlabTable::owner`), not the descriptor; with this change the descriptor is compared inside `release_owned`, so the message could name the owner word.
