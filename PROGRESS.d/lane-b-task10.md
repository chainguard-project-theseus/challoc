## Design decisions

- No limit is eager. `MADV_FREE` pages stay charged and in RSS until reclaim and would mislead Valkey's RSS fragmentation ratio and autoscalers (design 8 said so about `lazy_free`); the lazy advice is a saving the policy grants only where it knows the headroom (`Low` band). Recorded in design 8 and the design 11 table's new advice column.
- Sticky zero flag instead of a per-slab advice bit: `purged_is_zero()` turns false at the first lazy batch and stays false; `calloc` then clears `Purged` memory it reuses (a `memset` on mostly-resident pages), `Fresh` stays known zero, `malloc` unaffected. Chosen because the per-slab form needs a descriptor bit read in `class::take`/`huge::take` (not lane B's files) for a saving bounded to `calloc` on `Purged` reuse; the protocol rows do not move. Recorded in design 8.
- Explicit purges are eager whatever the band (`purge_all`/`purge_arena` hold an `Explicit` guard): `malloc_trim(0)` and `arena.<i>.purge` mean "give RSS back now", and the `high`-rise full purge runs because the kernel is reclaiming. `lazy_free:true` still wins there (decay.c `lazy` mode unchanged).
- Advice keys live under `stats.cgroup.*` (the plan's wording) but read without a tracked cgroup, unlike their siblings: the advice exists either way (eager, or lazy under `lazy_free`).
- The new key is `lazy_band` (bool) rather than a tri-state `lazy_free`: `opt.lazy_free` stays a bool for jemalloc-shaped readers.
