## Design decisions

- `auto` refuses where the PMD size is `madvise`-gated (`hugepages-2048kB/enabled` `madvise`, or `inherit` under a `madvise` top level, Ubuntu's default). The same `MADV_HUGEPAGE` that enables the 64 KiB folio would make the region eligible for 2 MiB pages at a fault over an untouched aligned range and, slowly, through khugepaged (`max_ptes_none` 511 collapses any range with one present page), and `auto`'s promise is no 2 MiB page. The host requirement is therefore `hugepages-64kB/enabled=madvise` (or `always`) with `hugepages-2048kB/enabled=never` (or `always`, the host's own choice for every process); recorded in design 17 and the results file. On Ubuntu's default sysfs `auto` is inert and the report says why.
- `always` commits and advises whole PMD-sized chunks rather than advising per slab: the VMA grows one slab at a time, so a per-slab advice would give 2 MiB pages only through khugepaged (16 MiB per 10 s), invisible in a two-minute row and unlike jemalloc's 2 MiB extents; the chunk makes the first touch a 2 MiB fault. Every slab of a chunk re-issues the two calls (a microsecond each, no-ops after the first), since groups are 1..16 slabs and arenas commit theirs in any order; the counters take the call that starts the chunk.
- Guard slabs inside a chunk are left unused and unguarded under `always`: a guard marker (or a `PROT_NONE` page) inside a PMD-mapped page splits it, which the first form did to every chunk. The trade is recorded in design 5 and 17; `auto` keeps the guards (its folio is the slab itself).
- Metadata follows `thp` (no separate `metadata_thp`, which stays an accepted jemalloc key): tables are folio-aligned at reservation and their commits rounded to the folio, so the first touch maps one folio; never a 2 MiB page for the metadata (a table's first page would cost 2 MiB of RSS). The review's `MADV_COLLAPSE`-for-hot-metadata idea is therefore probe-only: the advice at commit makes a copy afterwards unnecessary, and the syscall cannot collapse below the PMD through 7.0.
- The `MADV_COLLAPSE` probe runs only when `thp` is not `never` and the line says `collapse=unprobed` by default: populating and collapsing 2 MiB in every process's init is not a cost the default configuration should pay for a reported-only fact.
- `auto` is not forced to `never` under a cgroup limit below 1 GiB (design 11's rule stays for `always`): a 64 KiB folio is the slab's own granularity and the metadata rounding is bounded by the tables in use (+0.1% RSS on the 500 MiB heap, +2 MiB on a 16 MiB one).
- Default `never`, `auto` an opt-in: the plan's third condition (scorecard-shaped rows do not lose) fails on larson (1.6..1.7%, disjoint lists) and on small-heap RSS, while the target rows are flat; the numbers are in front of the human in the results file for a later reversal on aarch64 hosts that enable the knob.
