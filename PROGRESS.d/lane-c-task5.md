## Design decisions

- Section 18 is numbered after 17 and the source layout moved to 19, as asked. `src/sys/mod.rs:1` and `docs/plan.md:39` (Phase 0) still say "section 18" for the source layout; both are outside this lane's files, one-word fixes for the coordinator.
- The MTE default is `tag_check:async` as the plan says; the section states that async gives no faulting address (`SEGV_MTEAERR`), recommends `asymm` where `mte3=yes`, and makes `sync` the negative tests' mode. Reason: the plan's default is the cheap one; the address is a diagnostic the operator can switch on.
- Retag on free is the design (the plan's acceptance test "a read through a freed pointer faults" needs it); the variant that tags at pop only is written down for part 2 to price next to it, with what it gives up. Reason: the retag is `zero_on_free`'s line fills, the pop-only sweep touches lines the program writes anyway; only measurement decides, and the human decides after it.
- The cache holds tagged pointers, so the pop is untouched and the tag work sits on `free`, the refill and the pop of a zero-prefix slot. Reason: the pop is the four-instruction path.
- The software tag is six bits at 57 on both architectures (LAM_U57's bits) and `lam` on aarch64 means TBI. Reason: one encoding, one mask table; TBI makes the whole software-tag half of part 2 runnable on the reference C4A now.
- The tag map is one byte per slot (relaxed load/store, no RMW) rather than nibbles. Reason: two owners of one slot is the double free the compare catches; a nibble would need an RMW on a byte two slots share.
- The sized path verifies the tag at the call under the key (one byte load and store), giving up 6.3's "no metadata load per sized free" only when the key is on. Reason: the key exists to check pointers; the bit test stays deferred.
- `challoc_features()` does not gain a `tagging=` key; the effective mode is a `malloc_stats_print` line with the reason. Reason: the line is built in init step 1 before the configuration is parsed and reports hardware, not decisions.
- No `src/` change and no results file: the task is design, and the task 4 matrix already holds the probe results per CPU (the plan's acceptance names the matrix file).

## Notes for the human

- No reference VM and no GCE CPU can run MTE or LAM (design 18.1). What is not gated: the software tag over TBI on aarch64, implementable and priceable on the C4A today; it covers the stale-tag, forged and cross-class negative tests, the fuzzer and suites under the key, and the arm64 cost rows. The x86_64 half is one mask and one `arch_prctl` away when a LAM host exists.
- Cheapest MTE/LAM correctness: QEMU TCG (`-M virt,mte=on -cpu max` for aarch64; `-cpu max` with a 6.4+ guest for LAM, QEMU 8.1+) on the Mac container or a GCE VM; no numbers from it.
- MTE numbers need an AmpereOne bare-metal host (Ampere's documentation confirms MTE to Linux user space there; Oracle's A2 shapes unchecked in a guest). Graviton4 (`c8g`) and Cobalt 100 (`Dpsv6`) are each a `grep mte /proc/cpuinfo` on the smallest instance away; expect no.
- LAM numbers need a Granite Rapids / Sierra Forest host with kernel 6.4+ or a guest whose hypervisor passes `lam` (KVM 6.8+); AWS's 8th-generation Intel shapes are the candidates to check. UAI: nothing until Linux has an enable path.
- Section references to fix outside the lane: `src/sys/mod.rs:1` and `docs/plan.md:39` say "design.md section 18" for the source layout; it is 19 now.
