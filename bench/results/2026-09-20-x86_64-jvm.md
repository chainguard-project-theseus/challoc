- Throughput: challoc served 3.2% fewer requests than glibc on both
  endpoints (84119 / 47503 against 86812 / 49028 req/s) at the same CPU

  chases it, the place to look is the cross-thread free: every 64 KiB
  direct buffer is allocated by one of the 200 executor threads and freed
  by the JVM's single Cleaner thread after a GC, so under challoc the
  Cleaner's frees all land in slabs its own cache never refilled from

  show the same shape).
