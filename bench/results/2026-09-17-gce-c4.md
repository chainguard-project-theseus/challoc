## perf stat, larson 1 and 10 threads, challoc vs jemalloc

Phase 1.5 task 3 counters, taken right after the protocol above on the same VM
and commit, method of `bench/results/2026-09-17-gce-review-counters.md`: the
benchmark runs detached in the dev container (`sleep 1.5` then `exec`), `sudo
perf stat -e cycles,instructions,branch-misses,L1-dcache-load-misses,dTLB-load-misses,iTLB-load-misses
--inherit -p <container init pid>` on the VM host counts the whole container;
two rounds per (program, allocator); `larson 5 8 1000 5000 100 4141 <threads>`.
The six events share the counters with one multiplexed out (83% running time;
iTLB 67%), so perf scales the counts. Per-operation columns divide by the
operations larson reports (throughput x 5 s); compare allocators within a
program on those columns. `M ops/s` is larson's own throughput for that run.

Event semantics on the Xeon: `dTLB-load-misses` is `DTLB_LOAD_MISSES.MISS_CAUSES_A_WALK` (page walks; not comparable with the Arm column), `L1-dcache-load-misses` is `L1D.REPLACEMENT`. 16 vCPUs are 8 cores x 2 hyperthreads, so the 10-thread rows share cores.

Reading: at 1 thread challoc is faster than jemalloc here (48.4 vs 44.9 M ops/s, 68.1 vs 73.0 cycles/op) despite 7% more instructions per operation, with fewer branch misses (0.11 vs 0.49 per thousand) and the same L1D misses per operation (2.08 vs 2.10). At 10 threads on 8 physical cores challoc's L1D misses per operation rise to 2.85 (jemalloc 2.20) and cycles per operation to 131.5 vs 112.9 (+16.5%); IPC drops from 3.17 to 1.65. The dTLB walks per operation (0.08 vs 0.00) and iTLB misses also appear only in the 10-thread row. As on the Arm VM this is the cross-thread slab sharing of review P2 (Phase 1.5 task 4); the review counters at dacbedd had 125.7..127 cycles/op and 2.67 L1D/op for challoc at 10 threads, so the compacting cache and the slab geometry cost about 4% here at 10 threads and were neutral at 1 thread (66.8..68.9 -> 68.1 cycles/op).
