## perf stat, larson 1 and 10 threads, challoc vs jemalloc

Phase 1.5 task 3 counters, taken right after the protocol above on the same VM
and commit, method of `bench/results/2026-09-17-gce-review-counters.md`: the
benchmark runs detached in the dev container (`sleep 1.5` then `exec`), `sudo
perf stat -e cycles,instructions,branch-misses,L1-dcache-load-misses,dTLB-load-misses,iTLB-load-misses
--inherit -p <container init pid>` on the VM host counts the whole container;
two rounds per (program, allocator); `larson 5 8 1000 5000 100 4141 <threads>`.
The six events share the counters with one multiplexed out (83% running time;
iTLB 67%), so perf scales the counts. Per-operation columns divide by the
operations larson reports (throughput x 5 s); compare allocators within a
program on those columns. `M ops/s` is larson's own throughput for that run.

Event semantics on Neoverse V2: `dTLB-load-misses` is `L1D_TLB_REFILL` (most refills hit the 2048-entry L2 TLB), `L1-dcache-load-misses` is `L1D_CACHE_REFILL`.

Reading: at 1 thread challoc runs 5.6% more instructions per operation than jemalloc (235.6 vs 223.2), 2.49 vs 2.06 L1D refills and 3.55 vs 1.75 L1 dTLB refills per operation, for 4.4% more cycles per operation (69.1 vs 66.2). At 10 threads challoc's instruction count is unchanged (236.8/op) while its L1D refills rise to 3.10/op (jemalloc 2.23) and dTLB refills to 4.0/op (jemalloc 1.9), and cycles per operation to 86.6 vs 77.5 (+11.8%), which is the 10-thread larson gap: cross-thread sharing of slabs under the single default arena (review P2, Phase 1.5 task 4). Compared with the review counters at dacbedd, the compacting cache and the slab geometry added about 4 instructions and 0.35 L1D refills per operation at 1 thread (231.4 -> 235.6 instr/op, 2.15 -> 2.49 L1D/op); the 10-thread figures moved from 83.3 to 86.6 cycles/op and 2.90 to 3.10 L1D/op.
