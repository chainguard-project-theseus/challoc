### 1.3 The second failure: `c_thp_always` on 64 KiB pages

`api::ctests::c_thp_always` (task 9, lane B) fails on the 64 KiB kernel
(reproduced on a kept lab cell, `matrix/2026-09-19-lane-d-acceptance-64k-arm64-thp.log`):
at 64 KiB pages the PMD size is 512 MiB (`hugepages-524288kB` `inherit`
under a `madvise` top level, every smaller size `never`, so the feature
line reads `thp=madvise mthp=512M`); `MALLOC_CONF=thp:always` takes mode 2
and commits and advises PMD-sized chunks (`class_ranges 5 class_bytes
1409286144`, 1.3 GiB of `MADV_HUGEPAGE` ranges for the test's 8 MiB of
64-byte blocks), the VMA has `hg` but `THPeligible 0` and `AnonHugePages 0
kB` (the kernel does not give a 512 MiB folio to an 8 MiB touch), and the
test's two assertions, written for a 2 MiB PMD ("THPeligible 0 with hg and
2M enabled"), fail. `thp` is off by default (`never`), so the floor with
defaults is untouched; the test suite is red at 64 KiB and `thp:always`
should refuse (or fall back to folios) where the PMD is not 2 MiB. Lane B's.
`c_thp_never` and `c_thp_auto` pass there (auto inert: no size below the
PMD enabled).


### 1.2 The one failure: the container churn on the Debian kernels

On every Debian cell (5.10 x86_64, 6.1 x86_64, 6.1 arm64) `tests/c/cgroup_churn.c`
`purge 400 100` under `--memory=512m` fails: RSS falls from 403 MiB to
309..349 MiB and stays there for the 2 s window (criterion: under 100 MiB),
`stats.cgroup.empty_bytes` 8 MiB, `full_purges 0`. On the two x86 e2 cells
it fails in `make test` and again in `make test-cgroup` (4 of 4 runs); on
the arm64 c4a cell `make test`'s run passed and `make test-cgroup`'s
failed. The same cells passed the same test this morning at d152a64
(`2026-09-19-matrix.md`).

Attribution on a kept 6.1 arm64 lab cell (`challoc-lane-d-lab-k61-arm64`,
`6.1.0-53-cloud-arm64`, Debian's THP `always`; log
`matrix/2026-09-19-lane-d-acceptance-k61-arm64-churn-bisect.log`), the
same docker command as the cell, repeated:

| configuration | THP (host) | ok / runs | failing runs' RSS at 2 s |
|---|---|---:|---|
| default (675f357) | `always` | 6 / 8 | 309, 309 MiB (`empty_bytes` 8) |
| `lazy_band:false` (the `challoc-eager` allocator of `scripts/cgroup-tests.sh`) | `always` | 8 / 8 | |
| default | `madvise` | 7 / 8 | 216 MiB (`empty_bytes` 1) |
| `lazy_band:false` | `madvise` | 4 / 4 | |
| default at 9157450 (the tree before task 10; `matrix/...-churn-old-9157450.log`) | `always` | 12 / 12 | |

So: 13 of 16 default runs pass and 3 fail; 12 of 12 with `lazy_band:false`
and 12 of 12 at the pre-task-10 tree pass; the THP setting is not the term.
The mechanism is task 10's two-advice purge (lane B, e541172/767578a): in
the failing runs the freed memory *is* purged by the allocator
(`empty_bytes` 1..8 MiB, the slabs are `Purged`) but with `MADV_FREE`,
whose pages stay resident and charged until the kernel reclaims them
(`2026-09-19-lane-b-two-advice-purge.md` says so for the band's design),
and a 512 MiB cgroup at 60% usage is under no reclaim pressure. The churn
frees at 403 of 512 MiB (79%), above the 70% line of the `Low` band, so
the advice should have been `MADV_DONTNEED`; `purge::set_headroom` mirrors
the band only when `policy::evaluate` runs on a sample (rate-limited to
10 ms, on the tick), so the first flush after the frees can read the band
of an earlier sample taken while the working set was still under 358 MiB.
That the rate is 3/16 on a 4-vCPU Axion and 4/4 on the 2-shared-core e2
cells, and 0 in lane B's own runs on the 16-vCPU reference VMs, fits a
sample that does not keep up with the churn's 400 MiB ramp on a slow host.
Which of the two readings (a stale mirror, or the band itself) is the
whole story is lane B's to settle in `src/purge.rs`/`src/policy.rs`; the
churn criterion (RSS under 100 MiB within 2 s) cannot be met by any lazy
purge, so either the eager band must be what the churn's flush sees, or
the criterion accepts `memory.stat pglazyfree` as purged. Until then the
floor is not green on 5.10 and 6.1.
