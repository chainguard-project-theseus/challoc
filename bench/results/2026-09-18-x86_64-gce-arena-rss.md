## Reading and probe (x86_64)

The attribution, the probe method (`bench/probes/arenasample.c`) and the
change are in `2026-09-18-aarch64-gce-arena-rss.md`; this VM's A/Bs of the
change are `2026-09-18-x86_64-gce-arena-rss-fix.md` (first tree) and
`-fix2.md` (final tree, commit d10fb6d). The narenas A/B above: `n4` costs
larson16 -12.4% against jemalloc (286 vs 327 M ops/s), `n8` -7.8% with
mstress +31% and alloc-testN +15% RSS, so no arena count passes every row
here either.

Probe of mstress with the final library, one run each (MiB):

| conf | maxrss/VmRSS | smaps classes | smaps meta | smaps huge | small partial waste (peak) | small dirty (peak) | small dirty (exit) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| narenas:1 | 286.4 | 120..124 | 1.2 | 149..160 | 1.1 | 8.1 | 24.3 |

jemalloc's protocol maxrss is 285. After the change the remaining +18.5%
(fix2 A/B: 338.0 against 285.2) is three per-arena terms of the small
classes, none of them the caches or the tables the change removed: Empty
64 KiB slabs of the small classes waiting out the full `dirty_decay_ms`
window in every arena (+29 MiB; design section 8 purges a slab only once
it is older than the whole window, where jemalloc's smoothstep curve purges
about half of a window's dirty pages by mid-window), one partial slab per
small class per arena (+15 MiB of committed slab space, part of it
touched), and the foreign caches' slot stacks in the metadata region (16
threads x 16 arenas x the classes mstress's transfers touch, +9 MiB of
`challoc:meta`). On aarch64 the same run is +8.9% (318.7 against 292.6):
the VM runs mstress in 0.38 s per iteration against 0.62 s here, so less
of each arena's churn is in flight per window.
