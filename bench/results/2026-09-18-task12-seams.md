## 2. Attribution (aarch64, `quick-perf`, xmalloc, old = dd26516, new = the table form below)

| row | variant | M ops/s | cycles/op | instr/op | L1D miss/op | dTLB miss/op |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| xmalloc | old | 361.55 | 74.3 | 396.4 | 0.295 | 0.100 |
| xmalloc | new | 354.36 | 75.4 | 405.5 | 0.300 | 0.102 |

+9.1 instructions and +1.1 cycles per operation, misses unchanged: an
instruction-count cost on the cross-arena free path, where xmalloc-test's
consumers free every slot the producers allocated. `free_other` used to
compare `class` with the constant `NBINS`; the first cut called
`Config::cap_for`, which computes the whole capacity ladder (`ilog2`,
shifts, two clamps) to compare it with zero, on every such free.

## 4. Third form (final): no predicate on the cross-arena paths

`free_other` and `alloc_other` look the node up without creating it
(`other_caches(.., false)`) and push or pop; a miss (no node, no cache, a
full or empty cache) goes to `free_slow_in`/`alloc_slow_in`, whose
`ensure_cache` is the one site consulting `Config::cap_for` and creates the
node and the cache only when the capacity is non-zero. Fewer instructions
than dd26516 on the path (the `NBINS` compare is gone too), one rule.

the confirmation rounds' noise, xmalloc is 5..8% ahead on both

97.0% end occupancy) on both. Scorecard lines for 2a8c6b6 in
`scorecard-aarch64.md` and `scorecard-x86_64.md` (the x86_64 file also has
