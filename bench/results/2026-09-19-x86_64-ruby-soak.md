2. Where it is (final row, MiB): allocated 108 vs 97 (+11%: the slots held
   in 17 thread caches count as allocated in both, challoc's caches are
   deeper), active 219 vs 110 (active/allocated 2.03 vs 1.13), resident
   421 vs 140 (resident/active 1.92 vs 1.28), retained 64 vs 78. Two
   terms, roughly equal: 110 MiB of slab under-occupancy and 200 MiB of
   dirty (Empty, not yet purged) slabs.
3. The report names the mechanism. challoc built 17 arenas (`Arenas: 17
   (64 automatic)`): the main thread and each of the 16 workers got its
   own, and each worker arena ends the run with 5.6 MiB allocated, 12.3
   MiB active and 12 MiB dirty small memory, the same picture 16 times
   (`arenas[1..16]`). Per class over all arenas, the long-lived classes
   (1280..2048 B, the `l` strings and the 50..100-element arrays) sit at
   48..52% occupancy in 64 KiB slabs (12.8 MiB live in 26.1 MiB of slabs,
   15.2 in 31.6, 18.1 in 36.3, 20.8 in 40.1); the burst classes (2560..4096

   in 5.3..6.7 MiB of slabs). jemalloc's merged bins for the same sizes
   show util 0.83..0.91 for 1280..2048 B in 4..28 KiB slabs (`pgs`
   1..7) and 8..31 slabs left in the 2560..4096 B classes. The occupancy
   histograms (`arenas[1]`) spread across every eighth: the one-in-four
   long-lived object per job pins a slab whose other slots died. The
   compacting cache never engaged: `c_on 0` in every class of every
   arena, `gated` 7..93 per class (probes declined by the refill-rate gate
   under the bursty refill pattern).
4. Dirty. Right after a full GC each worker arena holds ~12 MiB of Empty
   slabs; class regions are per size class, so an Empty 3072 B slab
   serves only the next 3072 B burst, and the per-class inventory of a
   burst workload is the sum of per-class peaks. jemalloc's dirty at the
   same instant is 30 MiB in total (resident - active), its extents being
   class-agnostic. Both decay in 10 s; the periodic-GC samples show the
   steady state, not a lag.
5. `background_thread:true` (challoc-bg) is within -0.6..-2.0% of the
   default on every row, allocator stats included (resident 413 vs 421):

   and the thread has nothing to add. It is not the lever for

   tests the arena term directly.
